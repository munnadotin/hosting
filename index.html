Data Security Management

Security is defined before your team gives us access.

Our approach is simple: use only the access required for the assigned work, keep client information within approved systems, document responsibilities, and escalate concerns quickly. Controls are tailored to the client's environment and contractual requirements.

Client-controlled access. Defined handling rules. Clear accountability.

Where the client environment supports it, we work through named accounts, least-privilege access and multi-factor authentication. We follow client-approved systems and agreed rules for storage, download, transfer and retention.

We describe the controls we actually operate. Additional security requirements can be documented in the service agreement or SOP before access is granted.
01

Confidentiality & NDA

Confidentiality obligations are documented. NDAs can be executed before sensitive information is shared.

02

Named accounts & least privilege

Access is limited to the systems and information required for the assigned role, where supported by the client environment.

03

MFA & approved systems

MFA is used where available. Client information is handled through client-approved systems and communication channels.

04

Storage & transfer rules

Client data is not intentionally moved to unapproved personal storage. Download, transfer and retention rules are agreed per engagement.

05

Access review & offboarding

Access can be reviewed during delivery and removed or returned according to the agreed offboarding process when work ends.

06

Incident escalation

Suspected security or confidentiality issues are promptly escalated to the designated client contact and documented for follow-up.
